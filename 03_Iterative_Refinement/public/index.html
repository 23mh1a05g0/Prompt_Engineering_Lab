📈 Refinement Evolution Summary
Round 1 (Minimal)
Open-ended instruction yields variable depth, unchecked length, and general overview.
Round 2 (Constrained)
Enforcing a strict 2-sentence limit and target tone focuses the model on concise communication.
Round 3 (Anchored)
Requiring setting (Verona) and core themes produces the highest information density and relevance.
